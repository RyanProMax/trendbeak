## Calculation and coverage

For each position, marked value is original held shares × November 9 unadjusted close. The issuer's estimated capitalization is that same close × its dated reported common shares, with the separately disclosed AOS conversion-value exception. All 81 marked values remain in the weight denominator, including positions with missing financial fields.

For each positive-denominator included set, PB or PS is the sum of included marked values divided by the sum of (marked value × common book or TTM revenue / estimated issuer capitalization). Known nonpositive book is explicitly excluded from PB, not converted to zero or missing. Unknown weight is measured against the complete marked basket; at least 2% unknown makes that metric unavailable. Weight and coverage are recomputed for the new prices.

“Verified coverage” means coverage of the retained dated inputs under this method. Even 100% does not mean present-day financial accuracy, current shares or the current fund portfolio. HON revenue stays missing. DE segment revenue and AOS conversion value retain their exact original issuer, fact, definition and audit hashes. AXON has a new November 9 event-window admission in addition to the original November 6 source proof. No rank, historical percentile or alternative method is spliced into this series.

## Trusted source gate

The offline validator checks the exact approved November 6 input, registry and reconstructed checkpoint. It verifies all 81 raw quote bodies, original ticker/security mapping, currency, unadjusted close, date, New York regular-session boundaries and the unchanged prior close. A split, missing or duplicated price, revised prior close or changed identity stops the ordinary price-only path.

It also verifies all 81 original SEC submissions responses, equal-length arrays, issuer CIKs, bounded pagination and coverage from the financial anchor. Every observed new accession through each actual source-capture time needs a document-backed, independently reviewed disposition. Late disclosures are reviewed for earlier effective structural events; they are not ignored merely because they arrived after the price close. Their new financial values are not inserted into the November 6 financial anchor. Exact original-body hashes and supporting excerpts bind dispositions, rather than a candidate's claimed PASS or a filing form label.

For this observation, 45 accessions, including 10 post-close filings, are supported by 46 original documents. Ordinary capital flows are explicitly classified as dated-share estimation, not unchanged share count. Unknown financial fields remain missing; unresolved security identity, held quantity, split, spinoff, new common class or material scope change stops an ordinary roll.

The calendar evidence is the previously reviewed derived calendar JSON, not newly retrieved original ICE text. The attempted original calendar fetch failed and is not treated as successful evidence. Only the individually admitted November 9 normal session is enabled, together with the actual regular-session metadata in all 81 quotes. Unreviewed and early-close sessions stop before acquisition.
