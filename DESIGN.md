# trendbeak visual system

White canvas, thin separators and tabular figures remain the foundation. The brand is one continuous trend line folded into an open right-facing beak. Its outlined `trend` wordmark blends sage through muted gold to coral; `beak` is gray green. Dark-background and one-color variants preserve the same geometry. Existing market-chart colors remain; factor radars retain soft directional accents while volatility and activity use direction-neutral colors. Small text uses darker related tones for legibility.

## Calendar and quotes

Keep the original heatmap, shared month axis, keyboard navigation and trading-day rules. One continuous five-row weekday grid spans at most the latest 365 natural days. Weekend cells are omitted; weekday holidays and missing sessions keep distinct states. Uniform scaling preserves square cells and caps their size; labels thin independently and no horizontal scrolling or year splitting is used. Nearest-date hit testing supports dense grids. Grid height follows the square geometry; range changes anchor the dragged navigator in the viewport. Calendar and trend render precisely the same selected natural dates; no extra calendar-year boundary cells. The market name, instrument, point value, return and actual quote date now live in the left market area; mobile presents a compact header above each market grid. There are no standalone quote blocks at the top. A selected day updates these readings exactly; closed or missing sessions remain empty. The four-level return colors use softened green and coral from the same palette.

The calendar header contains only its title and the global selected date. Selection lives in the annual heatmap and trend chart; there is no separate date picker, adjacent-date control or latest button. The return scale and closed/missing keys share one centered, wrapping legend. Price dates remain per market: a common calendar selection is never used to pretend a market has a quote.

## Trend semantics and color

On first hydration use 365 natural days on desktop, 90 on mobile. Read the responsive default once; later viewport changes never reset it. Explicit windows survive refresh and same-tab reload in guarded session storage. The navigator has independent start/end handles, whole-window panning and background click-to-center. Its extent is the union of benchmark history clipped to the latest 365 natural days. Every resize, pan, restore and track jump obeys this cap. Version-one 604/730-day sessions migrate to version two and cannot revive. There are no preset buttons; start/end dates sit below opposite ends of the track. The navigator shows the selected start/end dates once; the internal inclusive day count is derived from this one window, not summed across markets. One absolute start/end pair controls both markets, with a seven-natural-day minimum (or all available dates if fewer). User-selected historical windows survive data refresh. Calendar selection and hover update cursors and exact-date readings, never the window, source paths or index domain. Out-of-window selection has a brief state.

Emotion color encodes its Y value, not elapsed time. The scale is flat green at 0–20, then continuously blends through gray green at 40, gold at 50 and apricot at 65, reaching coral at 80–100. A user-space vertical SVG gradient shares the fixed 0–100 scale; points and tooltip values use the same sRGB interpolation. This display scale does not change either source's scoring algorithm or verbal emotion categories. Benchmark prices use a distinguishable neutral solid line and a separate point-value axis.

Trend headers contain only the market name. Curve-end labels show numeric values only; their accessible names preserve the series, actual observation date and emotion category. Historical and hovered values live in a compact reserved readout below the curve, without a repeated date; the exact inspection date stays at the cursor. They never obscure the plot or change layout. Larger fixed-pixel axis labels, dates, legends and readouts share the calendar’s typography. Missing values stay missing; a lone CNN observation is a point, never a fabricated yearly line. Emotion is not gated by whether a price bar exists on that date.

## Motion and input

Monotone curves pass through real observations and preserve extrema. A fixed scene covering the available benchmark history holds the original paths and trading-day gaps; range changes continuously transform the clipped viewport and price scale over 420 ms. No intermediate observations are synthesized, and hover does not recreate paths. Reduced motion makes the transform immediate.

Mouse clicks on the SVG interaction region have no default rectangular outline. Keyboard focus remains visible through a thin, rounded gradient stroke. Other controls keep their own focus styles. The search field rotates a small conic-gradient border layer only while hovered or focused, pauses while inactive, and is static under reduced motion.

## Signals and validation

Four axes show Wilder RSI14 momentum, EMA20/EMA60 trend spread, Bollinger(20,2) width and RVOL20 activity. RSI uses its original scale; other coordinates use empirical midrank among the preceding 252 valid complete observations (minimum 60), excluding the observation being ranked. Raw values and units remain visible even when rank history is insufficient; no substitute midpoint. EMA is seeded with its first period SMA; Bollinger sigma divides by N. A live volume bar is not compared with complete days: the previous complete day and its exact rank are used, with its date visible only in the tooltip. Same-instrument reported volume is used; no ETF proxy. EMA color follows the raw sign, not historical rank; volatility/activity colors are direction-neutral. A light sage fill and subtle per-edge color transitions preserve the restrained brand. No weights, area score or fear/greed formula. These are different dimensions, not a claim of statistical independence. Missing coordinates never form a fabricated closed area.

There is no radar title, repeated permanent date, info badge, separator or annotation. Both real points and labels open the same temporary explanation and highlight. It follows the inspected point or axis, clears the diamond, raw labels and neighboring trend, and does not move layout. Chart text retains the default cursor; range handles use ew-resize and panning uses grab/grabbing. ARIA names replace native title bubbles. Touch pins; Escape, close and outside press dismiss.
